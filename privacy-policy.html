Privacy Policy

For 16 Guti Master — ষোল গুটি
Effective date: 11 November 2026

16 Guti Master (“the Game”) is a digital version of the traditional Bangladeshi Sholo Guti board game. This policy explains what information may be processed when you play the Game.

Information processed

  • Facebook Instant Games information: When you play through Facebook, the Game may request your Facebook Instant Games player display name and may use the platform's player-data feature to save and retrieve game session or progress data. The Game does not receive your Facebook password.
  • Game data stored on your device: When the Game runs in a browser or outside Facebook Instant Games, it may use your browser's local storage to save game settings and session data. This information remains in that browser unless you clear it or the Game removes it.
  • Platform and advertising data: Facebook and any advertising service made available through the platform may process information under their own privacy policies. The Game does not control the information practices of those services.

How information is used

Information described above is used to identify your in-game player name where supported, save or restore gameplay progress and settings, operate the Game, and support platform-provided features such as advertising where enabled.

Sharing

The Game developer does not sell your personal information. Information handled by Facebook Instant Games or an advertising provider is processed by that provider according to its own terms and privacy policy. The Game does not intentionally ask you to submit sensitive personal information.

Retention and deletion

Game session and progress data may remain in Facebook Instant Games player storage or in your browser's local storage until it is overwritten, cleared, or deleted. To request deletion of data associated with the Game, follow the instructions on our Data Deletion Instructions page.

Children's privacy

The Game is not designed to request personal information directly from children. If you believe that personal information has been submitted to the developer in connection with the Game, contact us so we can review the request.

Third-party services

When accessed through Facebook, your use is also subject to Meta's terms and privacy information. Facebook and any platform advertising providers operate independently of the Game developer.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
